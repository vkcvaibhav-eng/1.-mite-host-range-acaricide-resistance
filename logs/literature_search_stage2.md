# Literature search, stage 2 (13 November 2026)

Purpose: resistance cases for phytophagous mites in the three families that have no record in the Arthropod Pesticide Resistance Database. Rule (protocol): a bioassay resistance ratio of 10 or more against a susceptible reference, or a field control failure confirmed by bioassay; lab-selected-only populations excluded.
